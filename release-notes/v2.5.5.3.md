## v2.5.5.3 — Brave 1.93.138 Validation

**Patch release — Brave version bump only. No policy changes, no regressions observed.**

### Summary

v2.5.5.3 validates full compatibility with Brave **1.93.138** (Chromium **151.0.7922.173**, Stable, released August 21, 2026). All 150 policies across the 5 hardening tiers behave identically on the new build — no policy definitions changed and no regressions were observed. Brave 1.93.136 (Chromium 151.0.7922.137) and 1.93.129 (Chromium 151.0.7922.71) remain supported.
